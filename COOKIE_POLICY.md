# Cookie Policy

**Last updated:** October 3, 2026

The native Universal Mod Manager / MHW Manual Mod Manager Windows application does not set browser cookies and does not read browser cookies and does not embed a first-party advertising or tracking web view in the current release. Therefore, a cookie-consent banner inside the native application would be misleading and is intentionally not shown.

The app may open external websites in your normal browser or contact third-party APIs for updates, catalog/provider data, or other user-requested features. Those external services can use cookies or similar technologies under their own policies.

If the application later embeds web content that sets non-essential cookies or introduces browser-based tracking, the project must update this policy and add the appropriate consent/preferences surface before shipping that feature.
