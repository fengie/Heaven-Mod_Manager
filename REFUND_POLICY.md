# Refund Policy

**Last updated:** October 3, 2026

The current Universal Mod Manager / MHW Manual Mod Manager build does **not** process purchases, subscriptions, paid upgrades, tips, or other in-app charges. Because the project does not take payment through the application, there is no application purchase for the project to refund.

If you paid a third-party marketplace, distributor, hosting service, mod author, donation platform, or other provider, contact that provider and follow its refund policy. The project cannot reverse a transaction it did not process.

If a future release introduces a first-party paid product or service, a transaction-specific refund policy and any legally required disclosures must be added before accepting payment.
